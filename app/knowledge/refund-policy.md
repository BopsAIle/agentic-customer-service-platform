# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

Refund requests are generally considered after an order has been delivered. A delivered order may qualify when the item is damaged, materially different from its description, or returned under an applicable service promise. Eligibility is determined using the customer account and order records; this policy document is not a substitute for the business system.

## Timing and processing

Customers should request a refund within 30 calendar days of delivery. Once a request is accepted for processing, the review normally takes 3–5 business days. Payment-provider settlement may require additional business days after approval.

## Exceptions

Some final-sale, personalized, digital, or misuse-related purchases may be excluded. Support may request evidence or return information before a decision. The refund request tool and current order state remain authoritative for a specific order.
