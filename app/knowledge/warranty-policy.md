# Warranty Policy

## Coverage

Hardware and packaged goods may be covered by a limited warranty for manufacturing defects. Warranty review uses the original order record, purchase date, and product identifiers. This document is not a substitute for the business system.

## Period

A standard warranty review window is 12 months from delivery unless the product listing states a different period. Cosmetic wear, misuse, unauthorized modification, or missing proof of purchase may fall outside coverage.

## Remedy

Eligible warranty cases are typically handled by repair, replacement, or a refund when repair is not practical. Support may ask for photos or a return before a decision.
