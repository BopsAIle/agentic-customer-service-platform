# Shipping Policy

## Processing

Orders typically spend one to three business days in processing before carrier handoff. Processing times can be longer during promotions, holidays, or address verification.

## Transit and delivery

After carrier handoff, transit estimates depend on the selected service and destination. Tracking events may take time to appear after shipment. A delivery estimate is guidance rather than a guarantee, and carrier delays can change the expected date.

## Delays

For a delayed parcel, check the latest tracking event first. If tracking has not updated for several business days after the expected date, contact support with the order details.
