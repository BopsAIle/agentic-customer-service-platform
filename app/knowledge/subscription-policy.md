# Subscription Policy

## Billing cycle

Subscriptions renew at the interval shown at signup. The renewal charge is generally made at the beginning of the next billing period using the saved payment method.

## Cancellation timing

Customers may cancel before the next renewal to prevent future billing. Cancellation timing and access through the current paid period depend on the subscription terms shown in the account.

## Renewal changes

Plan changes may take effect immediately or at the next renewal depending on the selected change. Account billing records are authoritative for a specific subscription.
