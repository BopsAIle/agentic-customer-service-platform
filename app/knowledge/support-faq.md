# Support FAQ

## Contacting support

Customers can create a support ticket with a category and description. Include relevant order details when the question concerns a purchase.

## Account information

Keep contact information current so delivery and support updates reach the right address. Never share passwords or payment credentials in a support ticket.

## Order questions

Support can help explain order status, delivery questions, ticket progress, and general policy information. Specific order state must be checked against the customer-service system.
