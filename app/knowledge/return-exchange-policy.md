# Return and Exchange Policy

## Eligibility

Customers may request a return or exchange when an item is damaged on delivery, materially different from its description, or unused and in original packaging within the stated window. Eligibility is determined from the customer account and order records.

## Timing

Return and exchange requests should be made within 30 calendar days of delivery. After that window, support may still review warranty or exception cases but a standard return is not guaranteed.

## Process

If a return or exchange is accepted, support provides the next shipping or pickup instruction. Refunds for returned items follow the refund policy after the return is received. Exchanges depend on replacement stock.

## After shipment

An order that has already shipped cannot usually be cancelled in transit. Customers can refuse delivery where the carrier allows it, or open a return after the parcel arrives.
