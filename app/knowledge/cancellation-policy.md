# Cancellation Policy

## Before shipment

Customers can usually request cancellation while an order is being prepared or processed. A cancellation request is subject to the current order record and may not be possible if fulfillment has already advanced.

## After shipment

Once an order has entered shipment, cancellation is generally unavailable because the parcel is in carrier handling. Customers can instead follow delivery instructions, refuse delivery where supported, or contact support about an eligible return or refund.
